How it Works

A simple, effective line of defense against data exfiltration.

📝
1. Registration

Register sensitive text or files. We calculate a one-way Cryptographic Hash (SHA3-256) and never store the actual data.

🔍
2. Monitoring

Outbound traffic is seamlessly hashed in real-time. The system compares these hashes against our localized threat database.

🛡️
3. Prevention

If a match is found, the transfer is immediately blocked and the action is logged for administrators to review.

⚠️ Threat Model Addressed

This system simulates protection against Insider Threats or accidental data leaks. The attack vectors considered are:

  • Employees attempting to leak confidential files by uploading them to external services.
  • Accidental copy-pasting of sensitive text into public forums or messaging apps.
